Sunday Savings Bank

Connect the Google Sheet

The bank keeps its passbook in a Google Sheet, so every phone and iPad sees the same money.

  1. Make a Google Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script and paste in Code.gs.
  2. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app. Execute as Me, access Anyone.
  3. Copy the Web app URL and paste it below (or put it in config.js).
